Technology Leadership Roles We Place

We specialize in placing senior technology leaders who can scale engineering organizations, drive product innovation, and align technical strategy with business objectives.

Chief Technology Officer (CTO)

Strategic technology leaders who can architect scalable systems, build world-class engineering teams, and drive innovation aligned with business goals.

  • ✓ Technical vision & architecture
  • ✓ Engineering organization scaling
  • ✓ Board-level communication

VP of Engineering

Operational leaders who can scale engineering teams from 20 to 200+, implement best practices, and deliver complex products on time and within budget.

  • ✓ Team scaling & culture building
  • ✓ Process optimization & delivery
  • ✓ Distributed team management

Chief Product Officer (CPO)

Product visionaries who can define product strategy, lead cross-functional teams, and drive user-centric innovation that accelerates growth.

  • ✓ Product strategy & roadmap
  • ✓ User research & validation
  • ✓ Go-to-market execution

Chief Information Security Officer (CISO)

Security leaders who can build robust security programs, ensure compliance, and protect company assets while enabling business agility.

  • ✓ Security program development
  • ✓ Compliance & risk management
  • ✓ Incident response leadership

VP of Product

Product leaders who can manage product portfolios, prioritize features, and drive product-market fit in competitive SaaS markets.

  • ✓ Portfolio management
  • ✓ Feature prioritization
  • ✓ Cross-functional leadership

VP of Data / Chief Data Officer

Data leaders who can build data infrastructure, drive analytics-driven decision making, and unlock value from data assets.

  • ✓ Data strategy & infrastructure
  • ✓ Analytics & BI leadership
  • ✓ Machine learning initiatives

Why Technology Sector Expertise Matters

Technical Depth

We understand the difference between a CTO who can scale a Series A startup vs. one who can lead a public company through digital transformation. We assess technical depth, architectural thinking, and leadership style specific to technology contexts.

Market Intelligence

We provide real-time compensation benchmarking for tech executives, including base salary, equity structures (RSUs, options, phantom equity), and emerging components like retention equity. We know what it takes to compete for top talent in competitive markets.

Network Access

Our partners have 15+ years of embedded relationships in the technology ecosystem. We can reach passive candidates at FAANG companies, high-growth startups, and emerging unicorns who aren't on job boards but are open to the right opportunity.

Stage-Specific Assessment

A great CTO for a Series A company looks very different from a great CTO for a $500M revenue public company. We assess candidates based on your specific stage, growth trajectory, and strategic challenges.

Cultural Alignment

Technology companies have unique cultures—from engineering-driven to product-led to sales-focused. We evaluate cultural add, not just cultural fit, ensuring leaders who will expand your team's capabilities.

Speed Without Sacrifice

In competitive tech markets, speed matters. Our average time-to-shortlist for CTO roles is 21 days. We achieve this through pre-existing relationships and sector-specific networks, not by cutting corners on assessment.
