The Corlanth Difference

Why Sector Specialization Matters

Generic recruiters cast a wide net and hope something sticks. We take the opposite approach: we go deep in specific sectors so we can identify passive talent who aren't on job boards, understand the nuances of your industry's compensation structures, and speak credibly to candidates about the strategic challenges your company faces.

The result? Faster time-to-shortlist, higher quality candidates, and better cultural alignment. Our sector-focused approach means we can often present calibrated candidates within 21 days of kickoff.

Our Sector-Focused Approach

Embedded Networks

We don't cold-call strangers. Our partners have spent 15+ years building relationships within each sector, giving us access to passive talent who aren't actively looking but are open to the right opportunity.

Industry-Specific Assessment

We understand the unique competencies required for success in each sector. A great CTO for a consumer SaaS company looks very different from a great CTO for a fintech startup. We assess accordingly.
