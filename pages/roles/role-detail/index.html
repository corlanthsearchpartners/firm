VP of Engineering

📍 Remote (US) 💼 VP / SVP Level 📅 Posted January 8, 2026 🔒 Confidential Client
Apply for This Role

About the Company

Our client is a Series C SaaS company generating $80M in ARR, serving enterprise customers across financial services and healthcare. The company has experienced 3x revenue growth over the past 24 months and is preparing for a potential IPO within the next 18-24 months. The platform processes over 1 billion transactions annually and serves 500+ enterprise customers.

The Role

We are seeking a VP of Engineering to lead the company's 60-person engineering organization and scale it to 150+ over the next 18 months. This role reports directly to the CEO and is a member of the executive leadership team.

The ideal candidate will combine deep technical expertise with proven experience scaling distributed engineering teams, implementing enterprise-grade infrastructure, and driving platform architecture decisions at scale.

Key Responsibilities

  • Scale the engineering organization from 60 to 150+ across backend, frontend, platform, data, and security teams
  • Lead the platform's enterprise transformation, including SOC 2 Type II compliance and multi-tenancy architecture
  • Establish engineering best practices, CI/CD pipelines, and quality metrics across all teams
  • Partner with the CPO to align engineering execution with product strategy and roadmap
  • Manage a $15M+ annual engineering budget
  • Represent the engineering organization in board meetings and investor discussions

Ideal Candidate Profile

  • 10+ years of engineering leadership experience, with 5+ years at VP level or above
  • Proven track record of scaling engineering teams from 50 to 150+ in a SaaS environment
  • Experience with enterprise-grade infrastructure, high-availability systems, and multi-tenancy
  • Strong background in distributed systems, cloud-native architecture (AWS/GCP), and data platforms
  • Experience managing distributed teams across multiple time zones
  • Excellent communication skills with the ability to present to boards and investors

Compensation

Competitive base salary, significant equity package, and comprehensive benefits. Specific compensation will be discussed during the interview process and is commensurate with experience and market benchmarks.
