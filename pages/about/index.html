The Founder's Story

Why We Started Corlanth

After spending over 15 years leading talent acquisition for high-growth technology and financial services firms, I saw the same pattern repeat: companies were handing their most critical C-suite hires to large, impersonal agencies.

The result was always the same: slow time-to-fill, cultural misalignments, and junior recruiters learning on the client's dime. Corlanth was built to be the antidote. We operate with the agility of a boutique firm and the rigorous methodology of a global enterprise.

— [Founder Name], Managing Partner

Our Core Principles

01. Precision Over Volume

We don't blast job descriptions. We identify, map, and personally engage the top 5% of passive talent who aren't looking, but are open to the right opportunity.

02. Radical Transparency

No black boxes. You will know exactly who we are speaking with, what the market feedback is, and if a search is veering off track, immediately.

03. Partnership Beyond the Hire

Our success isn't measured at the signed offer letter. We actively support onboarding and check in at 30, 60, and 90 days to ensure long-term retention.
