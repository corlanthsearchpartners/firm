What It Means to Partner With Corlanth

Access to Unadvertised Roles

Many of the most transformative executive roles are never posted publicly. Through our retained partnerships with high-growth companies, we connect you with opportunities that match your strategic ambitions before they hit the market.

Strategic Career Advisory

We don't just send you job descriptions. We provide market intelligence, compensation benchmarking, and strategic guidance to help you evaluate whether a role aligns with your long-term trajectory.

Direct Partner Engagement

You will work directly with a senior partner who understands your industry, your level, and your goals. No junior recruiters, no generic outreach, no wasted time.

Ironclad Confidentiality

We understand that as an executive, your current position requires discretion. Our processes are designed to protect your privacy at every stage.

No Public Exposure

Your profile is never shared publicly or with your current employer. We only present you to clients with your explicit approval.

Controlled Introductions

You approve every company we approach on your behalf. We coordinate interview schedules discreetly, often outside of business hours.

Secure Data Handling

Your resume and personal information are stored in encrypted systems and shared only with authorized decision-makers at client companies.
