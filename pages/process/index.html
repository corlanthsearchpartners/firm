Not Speed at Any Cost.

Our 7-step methodology balances rigor with agility. We move quickly when the market demands it, but never at the expense of assessment quality or cultural alignment.

The Corlanth Search Process

01

Discovery & Strategic Alignment

We begin with a deep-dive session to understand not just the role, but the strategic context. What problem is this hire solving? What does success look like at 12 and 24 months? How does this role interact with the existing leadership team?

Deliverable: A calibrated position specification that goes beyond skills to include cultural add, leadership style, and strategic mandate.

02

Market Mapping & Intelligence

Using our sector-specific networks and proprietary research, we identify the top 50-100 potential candidates in the market. This includes passive talent who aren't actively looking but may be open to the right opportunity, as well as active candidates from our curated pipeline.

Deliverable: A comprehensive market map with compensation benchmarks, talent availability analysis, and competitive landscape insights.

03

Discreet Outreach & Engagement

We personally reach out to identified candidates with a tailored narrative about the opportunity. For passive candidates, this is a conversation, not a pitch. We emphasize confidentiality, strategic impact, and alignment with their career trajectory.

Deliverable: Initial engagement report with candidate interest levels and preliminary fit assessment.

04

Rigorous Assessment & Vetting

Interested candidates undergo a multi-stage assessment process: behavioral interviews focused on leadership competencies, technical depth validation (where applicable), reference checks with former direct reports and peers, and cultural alignment evaluation.

Deliverable: Detailed candidate profiles with assessment summaries, strengths/development areas, and fit rationale.

05

Calibrated Shortlist Presentation

We present 3-5 thoroughly vetted candidates, not 20 resumes. Each candidate comes with a detailed profile, our assessment notes, and a clear rationale for why they're a strong fit. We facilitate a debrief session to align on feedback and next steps.

Deliverable: Shortlist presentation with candidate profiles and interview coordination.

06

Offer Management & Negotiation

We guide both parties through the offer process, ensuring expectations are aligned and negotiations are productive. We provide market intelligence on compensation structures, equity benchmarks, and competitive offers to help you close top talent.

Deliverable: Successful offer acceptance with clear start date and onboarding plan.

07

Onboarding Support & Retention Follow-Up

Our partnership doesn't end at the signed offer. We check in with both the client and the candidate at 30, 60, and 90 days to ensure smooth integration, address any early challenges, and confirm long-term fit. For retained searches, this is included in our fee.

Deliverable: 90-day retention report and ongoing talent advisory relationship.

What Sets Our Process Apart

Speed Without Sacrifice

Our average time-to-shortlist is 21 days. We achieve this through pre-existing relationships and sector-specific networks, not by cutting corners on assessment. We move quickly when the market demands it, but never compromise on quality.

Radical Transparency

You'll receive weekly updates with specific metrics: candidates engaged, response rates, feedback themes, and market intelligence. No black boxes. If a search is veering off track, you'll know immediately—and we'll course-correct together.

Assessment Rigor

We don't just assess skills—we evaluate leadership style, cultural add, strategic thinking, and resilience. Our behavioral interview framework is designed to predict long-term success, not just short-term fit.

Typical Timeline Expectations

Week 1
Discovery, position specification finalization, market mapping kickoff
Weeks 2-3
Candidate outreach, initial assessments, market intelligence report
Week 4
Shortlist presentation, client interviews begin
Weeks 5-6
Final interviews, reference checks, offer negotiation
Week 7+
Offer acceptance, onboarding support, 90-day retention follow-up

Note: Timelines vary based on role seniority, market conditions, and client availability. Contingent searches may move faster; highly specialized C-suite roles may take longer.
