Scaling a Series B Fintech's Leadership Team in 60 Days

How we filled two mission-critical C-suite roles simultaneously with strict confidentiality, achieving 100% retention at the 24-month mark.

The Situation

A Series B fintech company experiencing rapid growth needed to simultaneously fill two mission-critical C-suite roles: a Chief Financial Officer and a Vice President of Engineering. The company had recently closed a $40M funding round and was preparing to scale operations from 50 to 150 employees over the next 18 months.

The existing CEO, a first-time founder with strong product vision but limited operational experience, recognized that the company's next phase of growth required seasoned executive leadership with specific expertise:

  • The CFO needed deep fintech experience, including regulatory compliance, financial modeling for SaaS metrics, and IPO preparation expertise.
  • The VP of Engineering needed experience scaling engineering organizations from 20 to 100+ engineers, implementing enterprise-grade infrastructure, and managing distributed teams across multiple time zones.

The Challenge: Both roles needed to be filled within 60 days to maintain growth momentum, but the search required absolute confidentiality. The company was in a competitive market, and news of executive hiring could signal strategic shifts to competitors, customers, and the existing team.

The CEO had previously worked with two large recruiting firms and was frustrated by the lack of senior partner involvement, slow time-to-shortlist, and candidates who didn't understand the unique challenges of high-growth fintech.

Our Approach

We engaged with a dedicated senior partner who had 15+ years of experience in fintech executive search. The process unfolded in four phases:

Phase 1: Discovery & Position Specification (Week 1)

We conducted a half-day discovery session with the CEO and board members to understand not just the technical requirements, but the strategic context. What problems would these hires solve in the first 12 months? How would they interact with the existing leadership team? What cultural attributes were non-negotiable?

Deliverable: A calibrated position specification for each role that went beyond skills to include leadership style, strategic mandate, and cultural add criteria.

Phase 2: Market Mapping & Intelligence (Week 2)

Using our sector-specific networks and proprietary research, we identified 75 potential candidates for the CFO role and 85 for the VP of Engineering role. This included passive talent who weren't actively looking but were open to the right opportunity, as well as active candidates from our curated fintech pipeline.

Deliverable: A comprehensive market map with compensation benchmarks, talent availability analysis, and competitive landscape insights. We also provided guidance on how to position the opportunity to attract top-tier candidates.

Phase 3: Discreet Outreach & Assessment (Weeks 3-4)

We personally reached out to identified candidates with a tailored narrative about the opportunity. For passive candidates, this was a conversation, not a pitch. We emphasized confidentiality, strategic impact, and alignment with their career trajectory.

Interested candidates underwent a multi-stage assessment process:

  • Behavioral interviews focused on leadership competencies and fintech-specific challenges
  • Technical depth validation (financial modeling for CFO, system architecture for VP Engineering)
  • Reference checks with former direct reports, peers, and board members
  • Cultural alignment evaluation through scenario-based discussions

Deliverable: Detailed candidate profiles with assessment summaries, strengths/development areas, and fit rationale.

Phase 4: Shortlist & Offer Management (Weeks 5-6)

We presented 4 thoroughly vetted candidates for each role, not 20 resumes. Each candidate came with a detailed profile, our assessment notes, and a clear rationale for why they were a strong fit. We facilitated a debrief session after each round of interviews to align on feedback and next steps.

For the final candidates, we guided both parties through the offer process, ensuring expectations were aligned and negotiations were productive. We provided market intelligence on compensation structures, equity benchmarks, and competitive offers to help the client close top talent.

Deliverable: Successful offer acceptance for both roles within 45 days of kickoff.

The Outcome

Both roles were filled in 45 days, 15 days ahead of the 60-day target. The results exceeded expectations:

CFO Hire

A seasoned finance executive with 12 years of fintech experience, including two successful IPOs. Within 6 months, the new CFO implemented SaaS metrics dashboards that improved board reporting accuracy by 40% and led the company's Series C funding round, securing $75M at a 3x valuation increase.

Retained at 24-month mark ✓

VP of Engineering Hire

An engineering leader who had scaled a similar fintech platform from 30 to 150 engineers. Within 9 months, the new VP implemented enterprise-grade infrastructure that reduced system downtime by 95%, hired 40 additional engineers across three continents, and established an engineering culture that improved developer retention by 60%.

Retained at 24-month mark ✓

Long-term Impact: At the 24-month mark, both executives remain with the company and have been promoted to expanded roles. The company successfully closed its Series C round, scaled to 180 employees, and is now preparing for a potential IPO in the next 18-24 months.

— CEO, Series B Fintech
